# 0004 — Theming architecture

Status: accepted, 2026-10-01. Supersedes the sublayer table of `0001-cascade-layer-contract.md`.

## Context

A theme was a JSON file of literal token values. It could not carry a rule, a font or a component-level decision, and changing the accent meant editing nine tokens in each of three schemes. No color could be chosen at runtime.

## Decision

**A theme is a module.** `defineTheme` from `@slotted/theme` takes seeds, a recipe, token values on the three axes, component tokens, fonts and CSS rules, and is typed against the token contract and the component tokens the family stylesheets read. `extendTheme` builds one theme on another. `slotted-build-theme` compiles a theme module to `<name>.css`, `<name>.js` and `<name>.d.ts`; `applyTheme` compiles and applies one at runtime.

**Color is derived from seeds in CSS.** Five seeds (`accent`, `neutral`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`) are registered custom properties. Every color token is an `oklch(from var(--slotted-seed-*) …)` derivation with a lightness bound that depends on hue and chroma, so any seed keeps the contrast ratios; `evaluateRecipe` computes the same values in JavaScript.

**Schemes are scoped.** Each scheme block is an `@scope` whose nearest root wins; an element with `data-slotted-seeds` derives every color from its own seeds. `data-slotted-scheme="system"` follows `prefers-color-scheme`.

**Theme rules have their own sublayer.** The published order is `@layer slotted.reset, slotted.tokens, slotted.theme, slotted.components, slotted.theme-rules;`. A theme's rules are emitted inside `@scope ([data-slotted-theme="<name>"]) to ([data-slotted-theme]:not([data-slotted-theme="<name>"]))` and may select only `data-ui`, `data-part` and the public attributes, and read only tokens.

## Consequences

- Minimum browsers: Chrome 125, Safari 18 (measured: WebKit 18.5), Firefox 146 (2026-10-05; Chrome 119 before). `tools/harness/scenarios/theme-recipe-parity.mjs` and `theme-scope-nesting.mjs` take `--engine chrome|firefox|webkit|webkit-18` and verify the matrix; the header of `tools/harness/engines.mjs` says how to obtain each minimum build (Chrome for Testing 125.0.6422.141, Playwright `firefox-1509` from `playwright-core@1.58.0`, `webkit-2182` from the `playwright-core-webkit18` alias). Bisected on Chrome for Testing: the renderer crashes on the parity page in 119–121; 122–124 leave the four `--slotted-tone-*-solid-hover` tokens unparsed, a relative color whose origin is `color-mix()`; 125 passes both scenarios, as do 131, 137, 147 and 150. Firefox 146.0.1 and 155.0 and WebKit 18.5 and 26.6 pass both.
- A bounded color carries no trigonometry (2026-10-05). Firefox 146–152 refuses `sin()`, `cos()`, `sqrt()`, `pow()`, `exp()`, `log()` and `atan2()` over a channel keyword, and 146 crashes on `hypot()` of them, so the 18 tokens that carry a bound or mix from one were invalid, and solid fills and the focus ring painted nothing; WebKit 18 drops the unit of `calc(h * 1deg)` inside `cos()` and computed them 0.12 ΔE away. `boundColorCss` (`packages/theme/src/bound.ts`) reads the hue through `a` and `b` of an `oklab(from …)`: each harmonic is a Chebyshev polynomial in u = a / c and v = b / c, and the chroma rides in the alpha channel, since a root over `a` and `b` is refused too. The coefficients and `evaluateRecipe` are unchanged. A bounded color is opaque. The default theme's sheet grows from 179,044 to 200,554 bytes (gzip -9: 14,454 to 14,598 bytes); `dist/index.js` of `@slotted/theme` from 214,223 to 214,934 bytes.
- Every scheme is named outside the `@scope` blocks (2026-10-05). Firefox (Stylo, 146–155) shares one computed style between siblings that differ only in the root selector of an `@scope`, so a dark sibling of a light one, and a dark portal layer beside a light one, took the light values. `renderTheme` writes `[data-slotted-scheme="light"], …, [data-slotted-scheme="system"] { --_slotted-scheme-anchor: initial; }` in `slotted.theme`, which makes the attribute a selector the style cache checks; the declaration keeps esbuild and lightningcss from dropping the rule.
- `@slotted/tokens` and `@slotted/theme-default` are gone; `@slotted/theme` and `@slotted/themes` replace them.
- A family that adds a component token makes `pnpm --filter @slotted/theme generate` necessary; `packages/theme/test/generated.test.mjs` fails until it runs.
- The neutral grounds follow the `neutral` seed in `light` and `dark` (2026-10-02). `recipes.standard` gives `--slotted-surface`, `--slotted-surface-raised`, `--slotted-surface-sunken` and the dark `--slotted-surface-inverse` 0.22 of the seed's chroma, capped at the chroma sRGB holds at each ground's lightness, and the light grounds stand at lightness 0.985 rather than 1. The default theme's light page, card and field are `#f8fafe`, not `#ffffff`. `high-contrast` keeps achromatic grounds.
- Every token of the `neutral` seed in `light` and `dark` takes the seed's hue with no offset and at least the grounds' 0.22 of its chroma, capped at 0.06 and at the chroma sRGB holds at its lightness (2026-10-02). A line, highlight or fill drawn over a ground carries at least the ground's chroma, so the step from the ground points at the seed's hue. `high-contrast` keeps achromatic grounds, lines and inks; its `--slotted-text-muted` keeps the seed under the same gamut cap.
- The runtime path enforces the build's selector policy (2026-10-05), reversing §10.2 of the design, where `applyTheme` did not validate. `ruleErrors` in `packages/theme/src/rule-policy.ts` is the one implementation: `slotted-build-theme` calls it after lightningcss reads the syntax, and `defineTheme`, `extendTheme`, `renderTheme` and `applyTheme` call it on every rule given as text, with the same refusals and messages. A rule given as a URL is checked by the build only. The policy and the hook index `src/generated/hooks.json` ship in `dist/index.js`: 214,934 bytes and 33.70 kB gzip at 3e03c531, against 177,510 bytes and 24.50 kB before the policy.
- The policy reaches past §10.2 of the design, which governs selectors and reads (2026-10-05). `@scope`, `@import` and `@layer` are refused at any depth, with the line and the at-rule, because the compiler writes them; `processRules` no longer checks them and is no longer exported. A declaration that writes a private property (`--_*`) is refused, and so is a declaration outside a style rule, which would apply to the theme's root. `@property` may register only a property of the theme's own, never `--_*` or `--slotted-*`, because it is hoisted out of the scope and holds for the whole page. A `style()` query reads what `var()` may read, in a value and in `@container`. `&` outside a style rule is refused, as `:scope` is, except as the first compound of a selector that goes on to a hook, carrying only `data-slotted-scheme`, `data-slotted-density` or `data-slotted-seeds` (2026-10-06): the root holds the theme's own scheme, which no descendant selector reaches. `data-ui` and `data-part` take only `=` or no value. Attribute names match without regard to case, and escapes in function and property names are decoded before the checks.
- An invalid control is told from a valid one by width as well as color (2026-10-05). In `high-contrast` the control edge, `--slotted-tone-danger-border` and both rings are one black, so color alone said nothing (WCAG 1.4.1). The semantic base token `--slotted-control-invalid-edge-width` (the default theme: `--slotted-border-width-thin`) is an inset edge an invalid frame adds inside its border, at rest and under focus; `text-field` reads it through `--slotted-text-field-invalid-edge-width`. Every family that paints an invalid state reads it, through a family token where the family already names its invalid colors (2026-10-06): field frames as the same inset edge, carried past the inward ring of a focused `plain` frame; `checkbox` and `radio-group` as a border wider by the edge, and a checked invalid box as a line of the surface between its border and its fill; `rating` as a wider outline on its empty stars; `calendar` and an editing `data-grid` cell as a focus ring wider by the edge. Forced colors remove box shadows, so an edge drawn as one returns there as an outline inside the border, and a focused invalid ring is double; a checked invalid `checkbox` or `radio-group` option keeps the system's checked pair (`Highlight`) with its wider edge in `CanvasText`, because the theme maps the danger fill to `ButtonFace` and the invalid rules had painted fill, edge and mark in the page's white; `prompt-composer` draws its edge as a border on a pseudo-element, which keeps the frame's own elevation. `packages/styles/src/invalid-edge.test.mjs` enumerates the families and lists the eleven that state invalid without an edge, with the non-color cue each uses instead: `color-picker` marks its `text-field` input invalid, and `wizard`'s progress segments stay color-only beside the step list's cross.
